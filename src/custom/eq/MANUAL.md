# EQ: quick guide

## Bands
- **Add:** click an empty spot in the display. Where you click sets the type: a high-pass at the far left, then a low shelf, a bell in the middle, a high shelf, and a low-pass at the far right. Keep the mouse button down to place the band straight away.
- **Move:** drag a node. Left and right sets the frequency; up and down sets the gain (on cuts, the resonance). Hold Shift for fine moves.
- **Width:** scroll the mouse wheel over a node to change Q. On cuts it changes the slope; on tilt, the shape.
- **On/off:** double-click a node, or Alt-click it.
- **Type and channel:** right-click a node.
- **Remove:** right-click a node, or use the X in the control bar.
- Up to 16 bands.

## Listen
Hold **Control** to hear only what the selected band (or the one you're dragging) works on. The curve shows what you hear. Let go to return to normal.

## Control bar (`controls`)
Shows the selected band:
- power and X (remove) buttons
- MID / M & S / SIDE: which part of the stereo signal the band affects
- arrows to step through the bands
- gain, frequency and Q boxes: drag them, scroll over them, or double-click to reset
- band type icons

## Value labels
The numbers next to a node work like the control bar boxes: drag, scroll, or double-click to reset. Ctrl-click also resets (Cmd-click on Mac).

## Piano (`piano`)
A keyboard under the display. Click it to add a band tuned to that note; drag its dots to move bands.

## Captions under the display
- **reset**: removes all bands.
- **controls / piano / ranges**: show or hide the control bar, the keyboard and the labelled frequency ranges.
- **analyser**: click to switch between pre, post and off.
- **range**: zooms the display through 3, 6, 12 and 24 dB, then auto.
